Northwest Arkansas Pest Control Basics

Common Northwest Arkansas Pests

Ants, spiders, roaches, wasps and fleas are among the common pest problems homeowners may notice. Activity often increases around food, moisture, shelter and easy entry points.

When to Call a Pest Professional

If pests keep returning, appear in multiple areas, or you are seeing more activity despite cleaning and sealing obvious entry points, a professional inspection can help identify the source and the right treatment approach.

Keeping Pests Out Year-Round

Seal gaps around doors and utility penetrations, reduce standing water, keep food stored securely, trim vegetation away from the structure, and address recurring pest activity before it spreads.
